# Species_Soil_Moisture_Threshold
######################################################################################################################################################################
1_studied_site_and_species_info.csv---longitude and latitude, begin and end of measurement, and number of species.
2_site_species_threshold.xlsx---critical soil moisture threshold from the fitting between soil moisture and species transpiration during soil dry-downs in growing season by linear-plus-linear model, linear-plus-plateau model and concave quadratic model. The model with the lowest AIC value and statistically significant parameters (p < 0.05) was selected as the best model (columns of 'AIC_const', 'AIC_quad','AIC_seg').
2_site_species_threshold_peak_season.xlsx---critical soil moisture threshold from the fitting between soil moisture and species transpiration during soil dry-downs in peak growing season by linear-plus-linear model, linear-plus-plateau model and concave quadratic model. The model with the lowest AIC value and statistically significant parameters (p < 0.05) was selected as the best model (columns of 'AIC_const', 'AIC_quad','AIC_seg').
3_site_species_threshold_driver.xlsx/3_site_species_threshold_driver_peak_season.xlsx---climate variables (such as MAT), soil variables (such as sand fraction), stand characteristics (such as stand height), plant attributes (such as DBH) from SAPFLUXNET observations and their metadata or SoilGrids, and plant functional traits (WD, P50, Ks, Hmax and their corresponding references or sources).
